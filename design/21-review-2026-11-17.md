# 21 — Review 2026-11-17 (v0.2.6)

A read-only review of the plans and the code at 0.2.6 (`29cd30c`), in five areas:
- the plans: design/00–20 against the shipped code;
- the core query layer (schema DSL, types, codecs, builder);
- the core runtime;
- the kit;
- the add-ons.

Nothing was changed while reviewing. Every finding below is **open**.

**How findings were verified.**
- Most were reproduced with a throwaway script, against PGlite (PG 18.3) or a fake `pg` pool.
- *reading* means code reading only.
- No real PostgreSQL or PgBouncer was available. COPY and pooler findings therefore rest on wire traces or reading.
- Seven of the P0/P1 findings were re-checked against the source by hand (✔ in the tables).

**Priorities.**

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P0** | Loses or corrupts data, or reports success on a broken state |
| **P1** | Wrong behaviour, a lock or availability hazard, a valid change that cannot be generated, or a type that lies |
| **P2** | Minor or semantic |

Sound by the review's account, so there is no need to re-audit:
- connection acquire/release (no leak or double release found);
- the savepoint settings restore after `RELEASE`;
- identifier and literal quoting;
- `$n` numbering across CTEs and laterals;
- codec round-trips (one defect, Q2);
- mq/outbox/events claim and lease SQL (no double claim, lost job or duplicate fire);
- the cron CAS on `next_at`;
- context's ALS use;
- scratch-database cleanup.
