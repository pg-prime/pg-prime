New package: `@pg-prime/nestjs`, a NestJS module: the configured `Db`, a `TransactionHost`, `@Transactional()`, request settings and rollback-per-test. It depends on `@pg-prime/context`, peers `pg-prime >=0.2.0`, `@nestjs/common` and `@nestjs/core` (`^11 || ^12`) and `rxjs`, and versions with the `fixed` group.
